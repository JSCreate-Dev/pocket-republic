# Civic and farm family art provenance

Six new sprites generated with the built-in image generator on 2026-11-08. Each original sprite was a geometry/type reference; the approved golden house was the material/style reference. Existing sprites remain as fallbacks. Exported 1x/2x/3x sibling imagesets live in PocketRepublic/Assets.xcassets/Buildings/building_<kind>_golden.imageset.

ImageMagick export: tools/art/export_civic_family.py accepts --farm, --townHall, --clinic, --school, --police and --dock source paths. It trims transparent bounds, fits within the unchanged logical canvas with a six-pixel 3x top/side allowance, pads to bottom-center and exports RGBA scales. Art geometry is illustrative; game footprints/entrances remain authoritative and require phone alignment checks. These are fixed-angle sprites, not directional/rotatable assets.
